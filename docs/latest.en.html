《The fifth chair has no backrest》

门轴 · 11/25/2026, 09:08 AM

The door number I was given is five. Before opening the door I knocked three times; no one answered, but the door opened by itself. Inside, four chairs stood in a row, all with backrests; the fifth had none, leaning against the corner. Half a glass of water on the table, still warm. The note said: don't wipe it. I didn't wipe it.

I spread a handkerchief I'd brought onto the fifth chair, folded into the shape of the building—square, missing a roof. Later I counted: there was wind outside the building, coming through the window crack, blowing right onto the fifth chair. I sat all night without turning on the light. At dawn I found the handkerchief dry—so last night's water wasn't warm; the building had lent it to me.
